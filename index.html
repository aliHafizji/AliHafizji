Hi, I'm Ali.

Glad you are here.

I build boring, profitable businesses, and I try to do it ambitiously slow: big goals, at a pace that leaves room for the rest of my life.

Early Days

Born in the beautiful city of Pune. I was packed off to Dubai at a few months of age.

Dubai in the 80s wasn't the shining showroom you see today. It was hot and sandy. The 16 years I spent there were amazing. I saw the city transform right in front of me from a desert to a place that the world wants to call home. Even though it will never really be their home.

Middle Days

I moved back to Pune at the age of 16. Moving back was a huge change. India felt like a maze of tight circles and inside jokes I didn't know yet.

I finished high school, did the engineering thing, and got a computer science degree from PICT. Everyone else in my family went back to Dubai. I didn't. I didn't want a life that depended on visas and stamps.

Work Experience

I've been working for 15 years (and counting) and have either worked with a startup or built my own. I like the chaos of 0 to 1. And the discipline of 1 to 10. Travel tech, consumer tech, gaming, B2B SaaS, consulting and agencies is where I've spent my working life.

I raised venture money once, for a fintech business. Now I build boring, profitable businesses, like a $2M agency that never burned cash.

Somewhere in between, I wrote two books on iOS development. So I'm also a published author.

Now

Most of my working hours go to Wednesday, the company I co-founded. We work with owners of non-technical businesses, roughly $3M to $50M in revenue, to find where AI changes their numbers. Then our forward deployed engineers sit inside the business and build it, sprint by sprint, until the number moves.

Most of my other hours go to writing. On Craft Theorys I write about being ambitiously slow: building boring, profitable businesses with big goals, at a pace that leaves room for the rest of your life.

The rest goes to my family, swimming, the gym and fiction.
